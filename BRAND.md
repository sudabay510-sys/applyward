# Applyward — Brand identity

**Slug:** `applyward` · **Tagline:** A week-by-week application timeline for college and trade-school applicants.
**Status:** Early access. Checkout not live; founding list only. No payments processed.

## Positioning
Students, parents, and adults returning to school who want a clear weekly plan for applications and aid forms.

**Promise (honest):** Organizing help only. No admission, aid, or scholarship guarantees.

## Personas
- **First-gen student:** Nobody at home has done this before — you want the steps.
- **Busy parent:** You want to help without hovering.
- **Career changer:** Heading to a trade or community college program as an adult.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Choose** — pain: Too many options, no shortlist. → plan: A simple research sheet per program.
2. **Organize** — pain: Deadlines live in 6 browser tabs. → plan: One deadline sheet and weekly tasks.
3. **Write** — pain: The essay feels impossible. → plan: A small-steps essay sprint in your own voice.
4. **Submit & fund** — pain: Aid forms and recs come last-minute. → plan: Early reminders tied to official dates.

## Voice & tone
Supportive older sibling: clear steps, zero judgment, honest about what nobody can promise.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing (planned terms; not live)
- **12-week season** — $29 12 weeks, then $29 / 12 weeks unless cancelled
- **24-week full cycle** — $49 24 weeks, then $29 / 12 weeks unless cancelled

## Do
- Use readable, accessible type (Atkinson Hyperlegible).
- Speak to students and parents with equal respect.
- Show intro **and** renewal prices before any future charge.
- Label the founding list as “no charge today”.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Imply admission odds or guarantee acceptance, aid, or scholarships.
- Offer to write essays.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
